Plot:- 
    A man finds a Book/Diary. Every page contains a person's name, address biodata & DATE OF DEATH.
    Each page have the person brief details. The man become curious to find the people and whether
    the details in the dairy are true or not.

Plot:-
    A person who is living alone in his room thinking he is lonely start hearing a female voice.
    He doesn't know the origin of voice, initially he is scared of voice but eventually the voice 
    talks back to him and becomes his only companion.

Plot:- 
    "The Missing Hour" Everyday b/w 7pm to 8pm a man loses memory. His dress would be changed in
    that 1hr. Sometimes his dress turns dirty. He sees msgs/calls that he didn't ever remember. He
    doesn't have the memory of that one hr.


NOTE:- 
    The Mystery is not the Final Answer. It should be an unexplained element that creates tension,
    nut the ending should complete the characters emotional journey.

    => "Why is this happening?" -> We never Know -> but we understand what it does to the Person.
